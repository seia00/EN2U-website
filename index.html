Our Purpose

Why we exist

We believe English is a vital skill that expands opportunities and human connection. EN2U is grateful for access to quality education and aims to bridge the gap for those who do not have the same chances. By mentoring others, we seek to create a fairer future where everyone can pursue their interests globally through English.

What We Do

Small classes,
big impact

We are a youth-led non-profit based in Japan where 13–18 year olds with strong English skills mentor peers in underprivileged areas. We run practical, high-quality lessons via Zoom Premium with small class sizes — maximum 4 students per teacher — to maximize attention and growth.

Mentor Criteria

EIKEN Grade 1

Passed Eiken Grade 1 across reading, writing, listening and speaking.

TOEFL iBT 100+

If Eiken G1 hasn't been taken: minimum score of 100 on TOEFL iBT.

Interview & Demo Lesson

Pass a one-on-one interview and a demo lesson that evaluates passion, communication, and teaching ability.

Our 6-Month Curriculum
  • 1 Class discussions on diverse topics to build real fluency.
  • 2 Image description exercises to expand practical vocabulary.
  • 3 Real-time vocabulary collection after every lesson to consolidate learning.

Impact

What We Have Done

Gaza Soup Kitchen

Our most recent initiative involves supporting Gaza Soup Kitchen, a grassroots organization providing thousands of hot meals daily to citizens across Gaza. With over 100,000 Instagram supporters, they are a vital lifeline in the region.

We are proud to leverage our platform to amplify their humanitarian mission and connect our educational community with global causes that matter.

Guatemalan Literacy Project

We partnered with an NPO to teach underprivileged Guatemalan children practical English. We created image-first Google Slides, ran icebreakers to build rapport, and provided detailed feedback after every lesson to encourage reflection and growth.

These three pillars — accessible materials, strong classroom culture, and personalized feedback — reflect our experience and passion for teaching.
